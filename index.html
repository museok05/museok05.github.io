About

Hello! My name is Museok and I am currently in my fourth year of Electrical engineering at the University of British Columbia!

I am interested in circuit and electronics design, especially the detailed engineering decisions that turn an idea into a reliable system. I enjoy comparing design approaches, evaluating trade-offs, and understanding why one solution may be better than another depending on performance, reliability, cost, space, and manufacturing constraints. This portfolio documents the projects I build, the challenges I encounter, and what I learn along the way. I am particularly interested in applying hardware design to emerging technologies such as renewable energy, electric vehicles, and advanced electronic systems.
